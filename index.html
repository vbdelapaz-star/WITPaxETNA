Welding Instructors Training Utilizing SEABERY Welding Simulator

Participant Expectations and Pre-Training Needs Assessment

Welcome to the diagnostic and needs assessment platform for the 4-day intensive welding instructor capacity-building training program. This tool captures baseline competencies, teaching assignments, technical expectations, and learning priority gaps.

Purpose

Identify technical and pedagogical gaps to customize simulator practice and instruction during the 4-day training.

Target Audience

TVET Welding Instructors, Master Trainers, Curriculum Developers, and Assessment Specialists.

Duration

Estimated completion time: 15–20 minutes. Draft progress can be saved at any time.

Data Privacy

Data is stored locally in your browser session/storage. No external server tracking or unauthorized transmission.

Assessment Completion Instructions & Rules
  • Part I–VI: Complete background details, teaching profiles, confidence matrices, and open expectations.
  • Part VII (Self-Assessment Learning Needs): Rate both your Current Ability (1–5) and Training Importance (1–5). The application automatically calculates priority gaps (Importance – Ability).
  • Part VIII (Baseline Knowledge Check): Answer 10 multiple-choice questions regarding simulator integration and TVET assessment principles.
  • Offline Capability: All data stays stored in your web browser. Use the Export & Backup section to download Excel/JSON files.
